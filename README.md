# AdVeil

> Mute and blur ads on JioHotstar for a cleaner cricket experience.

AdVeil browser extension is designed specifically for cricket fans who stream matches on JioHotstar, aiming to enhance your viewing experience by muting and visually blurring advertisements. AdVeil lets you focus fully on the game by automatically reducing the distraction caused by ad breaks, providing a cleaner, more enjoyable cricket streaming environment. The extension integrates with JioHotstar's sports streaming pages, detecting ad segments and applying a combination of audio muting and customizable blur/fade effects over the video player. This ensures that advertisements become significantly less intrusive without interrupting live match content.

You can easily control the visual effects via an intuitive popup panel. The popup panel allows adjustment of blur intensity and fade level to suit individual preferences, enabling a personalized balance between ad concealment and video clarity. All settings are stored locally in the browser, thus ensuring your preferences persist seamlessly across sessions without sending any data externally.

AdVeil is built to avoid interference with the actual match content. When advertisements end and the live cricket action resumes, AdVeil restores the original audio and video quality, ensuring a smooth transition back to uninterrupted gameplay. This allows cricket fans to enjoy their favorite matches on JioHotstar without the usual distractions posed by ads, making it an essential tool for those seeking a cleaner and more pleasant streaming experience.

### Key Features:

- Intelligent Ad Detection: Utilizes network monitoring to automatically identify advertisements during cricket streams on JioHotstar.
- Automatic Mute & Blur: Applies real-time audio muting and customizable blur/fade effects on the video player during ad breaks, minimizing distractions.
- User-Controlled Filters: Provides an easy-to-use popup panel where users can adjust blur intensity and fade levels to their personal liking.
- Seamless Integration: Operates exclusively on JioHotstar’s cricket and sports streaming pages, ensuring no disruption to other browsing activities or content.
- Persistent Customization: Saves your individual settings locally, so your preferred ad-hiding experience is maintained across browsing sessions.
- Lightweight: Employs service worker architecture for efficient and low-impact management of ad detection and UI updates.
- Non-Intrusive: Automatically returns audio and video to normal as soon as the ads end, keeping match viewing smooth and uninterrupted.

### Privacy:

No personal data is collected. AdVeil extension does not send any data out of your device. User settings are stored locally in the browser storage.
